Scripts kept as the record of how the published numbers were produced.

They cannot run: their paths point at external drives that are no longer attached,
and eval_plate.py additionally sets no random seed. They are here because the
current scripts are reimplementations, and when a published number is questioned
the answer should be a file, not a commit hash.

  eval/lp/eval_plate.py          LP generation, the paper's own script
  eval/ccpd/eval_ccpd.py         CCPD generation, superseded by cellset_edit.py
                                 (which also writes the cells.txt that Region
                                 LPIPS needs; none of the published CCPD outputs
                                 came from eval_ccpd.py)
  eval/ccpd/eval_fid.py          FID, the definition the published numbers use
  eval/ccpd/eval_lpips_ccpd.py   Full and Region LPIPS, likewise

The last two are not merely superseded -- they are what the metric definitions
were recovered from. eval_fid.py leaves the ground truth at its original
resolution and uses batch_size=1 (required: pytorch_fid cannot batch images of
differing sizes). eval_lpips_ccpd.py resizes through torchvision's T.Resize,
whose default is bilinear. eval/eval_image.py now matches both; before it did
not, and reported FID 4.2167 where the published figure is 4.78.

Use instead:
  eval/eval_image.py             FID / Full LPIPS / Region LPIPS, all via CLI
  eval/eval_ocr.py               ACC / NED
  eval/ccpd/region_lpips_ccpd.py Region LPIPS with per-cell mask reconstruction
                                 from the cells.txt written at generation time
  eval/lp/infer.py               LP inference with --seed and --prompt_mode
  eval/ccpd/cellset_edit.py      CCPD generation, any cell set
  eval/ccpd/mixed_span_edit.py   CCPD, province and alphanumeric in one span

See docs/EVALUATION.md for the definitions and the numbers each produces.
